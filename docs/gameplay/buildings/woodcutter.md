# Woodcutter

A new settlement has one timber landing at (4880, 2480) on the north forest
edge. A single cart track ends at the sawmill apron. The stack of cut timber
and the worn working floor occupy the same buildable foundation. The old trail
ending farther east returns to the forest. The two canopy fingers meet there,
with matching impassable woodland beneath them. To build on the site,
select the Woodcutter, tap the green foundation and confirm.
The same landing remains when the Woodcutter is built, lost or rebuilt.

One Woodcutter may be built per settlement. Existing saves keep every standing
producer, position and purchased level. Another Woodcutter may be
built after the last one falls, on a valid timber site in that save's layout.
The replacement starts at level one. Legacy outside producers still draw aggro.

Cost 60 wood / 20 stone / 10 gold. Base HP 300. Base output is 4 wood/sec
with the matching-site multiplier of 1.25. Local upgrades are instant through
level 100 and show current and next production, HP and exact resource cost.
Level-one upgrade price is 50 wood / 25 stone / 15 gold. Later upgrade prices
scale by 1.15^(current level - 1), rounded up per resource.

With one timber site, local levels 1–4 produce 5, 7, 8.5 and 10 wood per
game second on that site. After level 4 each level adds 8% to the previous
level’s output. The higher rate applies to paid levels in existing single-site
settlements. Older maps with multiple timber sites keep their 8% curve at
every level.

Each age painting keeps the open timber rack, large cut log ends and saw bench visible at map zoom.

Its local panel shows the current age’s timber-yard painting, the timber produced per second at this site and the next paid level’s output and maximum HP. While the structure is being built, show zero current production, the completed rate and the remaining construction time.
