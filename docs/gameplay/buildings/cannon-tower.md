# Cannon tower

Construction costs 220 wood, 200 stone and 120 gold. A new tower starts with
650 HP, deals 110 damage per shell, fires every three seconds, reaches 560
world units and hits every enemy within 140 units of the impact. Show the
actual weapon range during placement.

The Workshop offers three global branches for this weapon. Round shot raises
shell damage, Burst shells increases the blast radius, and Loading crew
reduces the time between shots. Each branch has a current-age icon, rank,
price and current/next value. All existing and replacement cannons use the
purchased ranks. See [age and research rules](../upgrades.md).

A migrated cannon retains the maximum HP, range, damage and firing speed of
its purchased old grade and the original 140-unit blast. Elites and bosses
take the same damage as ordinary enemies caught in a blast.
