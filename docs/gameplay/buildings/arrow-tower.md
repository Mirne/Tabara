# Arrow tower

Construction costs 140 wood, 100 stone and 60 gold. A new tower starts with
650 HP, deals 30 damage to one enemy per shot, fires once per second and
reaches 460 world units. Show its actual firing radius during placement.

The Workshop offers three global branches for this weapon. Arrowheads raises
damage, Bow limbs improves reach, and Quick nocking improves firing speed.
Each branch has its own age-specific icon, rank, price and current/next
value. A purchase applies to existing and replacement towers. Arrowheads also
scales Rain of Arrows. See [age and research rules](../upgrades.md).

A migrated tower retains the maximum HP, range, damage and firing speed of
its purchased old grade. Ordinary enemy damage applies at every rank.
