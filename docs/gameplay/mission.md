# Mission victory

Warlords enter at 05:00 and 10:00 with increasing strength. At 15:00 a much
stronger final Warlord enters from the western entrance. The clock keeps running
and normal waves continue. The mission wins only when that specific boss dies
and the Main hall is still alive. The victory screen offers endless survival.
The clock alone and killing an earlier miniboss never award victory. Hall
destruction takes priority when both deaths happen in the same simulation step.

Persist final-boss identity, defeat and completion explicitly. Reload must preserve
a living final boss without spawning another. New unfinished saves beyond 15:00
do not win from the elapsed time. Already-completed old endless saves keep their
earned completion. Unfinished late legacy saves get a single final Warlord, with
no replay of all missed minibosses.

The map, economy, resource counts, building prices/upgrades, ordinary waves and
other unit stats are unchanged by this request.

Menu and briefing objectives must describe the final-boss kill, not a timed win.
