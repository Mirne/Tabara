# Worker

Decorative worker animation at completed Woodcutters, Quarries and Marketplaces.
Workers are part of building presentation, not independent simulated combat units.
They do not gather clickable resources, take damage, change income, or need player
orders. Preserve the existing walking cadence and reduced-motion setting.
They stay associated with their building and must not cause resource-layer flicker.

On the two-site layout the Woodcutter's worker moves between the sawyer's yard
and the timber stack. The Stonemason's worker moves between the cut blocks and
its building. Both use the apron in front of the building, turn at each end,
and begin after construction completes. The two painted work sites and their
short paths connect the producers to their geography across settlement ages.
At the northern timber stack and the southern cut face, workers briefly stop
before returning across the worn working floor. The open-sided saw shed and
outdoor hoist emit smoke when badly damaged.
