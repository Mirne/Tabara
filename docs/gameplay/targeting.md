# Enemy targeting

Enemies start at the west entrance and seek the Main hall via passable terrain.
They destroy intervening buildings and walls, and retain retaliation against
reachable firing defenses. No crossing red cells, forest/resource cores or water.
All outside woodcutters, quarries, and passive-gold Marketplaces must be detected and approached proactively,
not merely attacked by an enemy already beside one particular deposit. Increase
producer detection range to 1600 world units; periodically acquire the nearest
reachable producer and retarget after it is destroyed. Use the existing terrain
pathfinder and attack walls that block the chosen route. Do not grant knowledge
of distant producers behind the base, or ignore collision to reach a deposit.
Ordinary damage applies to every enemy type. Keep existing speeds, health, damage,
spawning, kill rewards and wave progression outside these targeting changes.
