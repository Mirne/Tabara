# Woodland basin environment direction

The map is one temperate woodland ecosystem. Its composition starts with the
relationship between dense forest, rock, water and usable clearings. Decorative
object counts are not an acceptance criterion.

The central settlement occupies a broad connected clearing. A western approach
threads between forest margins and reaches the existing defensible pass. Dense
woodland belts enclose the northwestern and southwestern meadows, continue along
the northern lake shore, and frame the southeastern expansion area. Forest edges
open into deliberate timber-working pockets. The stone deposits read as exposed
faces of the same grey, mossy geology as the nearby escarpments. Shoreline plants,
pebbles and damp ground follow the water rather than appearing in unrelated spots.

All environment assets share one canopy family, stone palette, daylight direction,
view angle, and scale relative to the existing buildings. Forests are continuous
terrain masses. Ruined chapels, windmills, arches and solitary specimen trees are
removed from the new mission composition. A region must explain its neighbors.

Review the entire map and the initial playable clearing before freezing an APK.
Check forest density and edge continuity at gameplay zoom; resource pockets must
remain visible and usable. The visible terrain and the shared movement/building
mask must agree. Keep the existing mobile construction, repair, upgrades, controls,
resource limits and old-save compatibility.

Reference study: developer-published They Are Billions screenshots and full-map
views, used for landscape structure and playable negative space. No reference-game
art is included in the game.
